# Portfolio — Product Context

register: brand

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Alexandre Dissi (dissi.fr). Goal: present Alexandre as a
sharp, craft-driven software engineer, drive contact (email/LinkedIn) and
showcase Murmur, his flagship side project, as proof of ability to ship
end-to-end.

## Users

- Recruiters and hiring managers scanning quickly on desktop or mobile.
- Potential clients or collaborators arriving from LinkedIn/GitHub.

## Brand / Tone

- Quietly confident, precise, minimal. Same voice as murmur.dissi.fr.
- Monochrome canvas with a single indigo accent that echoes Murmur's
  lavender: the personal brand and the product brand rhyme.
- English copy. No em dashes.

## Visual decisions already validated by the owner

- Light #fafafa / dark #0a0a0b canvas, accent #4f46e5 (light) / #a5b4fc (dark).
- Centered max-w-2xl single column, hero glow + dot grid, timeline for
  experience, rounded-full CTAs, scroll reveals (CSS only, reduced-motion safe).
- Featured Murmur card with real OG image; description in English only.
- No "Open to opportunities" badge (owner removed it).
- Typography: Bricolage Grotesque for display (shared with Murmur),
  Geist for body, Geist Mono for meta/taxonomy (dates, chips, eyebrows).

## Strategic principles

- Sell through evidence (shipped product, real timeline), not adjectives.
- One page tells the story; subpages stay lean.
